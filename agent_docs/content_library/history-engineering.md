# Content Library — History & Engineering Documentaries

Grade 6 world history (ancient civilizations: Mesopotamia, Egypt, Israel, India, China, Greece, Rome) + how-things-work engineering. Class: `educational`.
Screening criteria: moderate language OK, violence OK, **progressive/leftist ideological framing is the disqualifier**.

## Acquisition notes
- **Physical media**: #1–6, 9–13, 17–20 all had complete DVD/Blu-ray releases; used sets cheap on eBay/Amazon.
- **Free/archive**: Macaulay specials, Secrets of Lost Empires, Trojan War circulate on YouTube/archive.org; Time Team official YouTube.
- **Curriculum map**: Mesopotamia → #5, #9(ep2); Egypt → #1, #6, #7, #12(Pyramid), #19; Israel → #6 (Kingdom of David); India → #3, #5; China → #1, #4, #5, #7; Greece → #1, #2, #6; Rome → #1, #6, #7, #8, #12(Roman City), #13.
- **Also consider**: Wood's *In Search of the Dark Ages* (1979–81) and *In Search of Alexander the Great*; Mary Beard's *Meet the Romans* (2012, 3 eps) as a lively Rome supplement.
