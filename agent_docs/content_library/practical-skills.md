# Content Library — Practical Skills

Woodworking, cooking/food science, farming/homesteading, general competence. Class: mostly `educational`/`mixed`.
Active builds: raised-bed garden (Q1), chicken coop (Q2), weather station (Q3), family cookbook (Q4).
Screening criteria: moderate language OK, violence OK, **progressive/leftist ideological framing is the disqualifier**.

## 1. Woodworking / Craftsmanship

**Best beginner trio for measurement + hand-tool discipline: Woodwright's Shop (early seasons) → Paul Sellers → Essential Craftsman.** Underhill for love of the craft, Sellers for slow precise technique, Wadsworth for jobsite competence and character.

| # | Title | Run / Count | Why | Projects |
|---|-------|-------------|-----|----------|
| 1 | **The Woodwright's Shop** (Roy Underhill, PBS) | 1979–2017, 37 seasons, ~468 × 30m | THE hand-tool show: no power tools, no shortcuts; S1–S5 teach lumber-from-a-tree fundamentals, layout, joinery with infectious joy | coop, garden frames |
| 2 | **Paul Sellers** (YouTube; yt-dlp) | 2011–, hundreds | Quiet master of marking-gauge/square/chisel discipline; beginner series (workbench, dovetail box) is the best "measure twice" modeling on video | coop, weather enclosure |
| 3 | **Essential Craftsman** (Scott Wadsworth, YouTube) | 2015–; Spec House series ~100+ eps | 40-year tradesman teaching foundations, framing, concrete, blacksmithing AND work-ethic monologues; Spec House = a complete house build, ideal long-arc viewing | coop framing/roofing, weather mounting |
| 4 | **The New Yankee Workshop** (Norm Abram, PBS) | 1989–2009, 21 seasons, 284 eps | One furniture project per episode, methodical measure-cut-assemble rhythm; models planning drawing→finish. Power-tool heavy (pair with shop-safety talk) | coop, cookbook stand |
| 5 | **This Old House** — Vila era S1–10 + early Steve Thomas | 1979–89+ | Older seasons: modest homes, sweat equity, real tradesmen explaining the WHY; understand structures before building a coop | coop |
| 6 | **Ask This Old House** (PBS) | 2002–, 20+ seasons | Single-skill 10-min segments (fix a gate, wire an outlet, pour a footing) — perfect channel filler | coop, garden, weather |
| 7 | **A Craftsman's Legacy** (Eric Gorges, PBS) | 2014–19, 4 seasons, ~50 eps | Each ep visits a master craftsman; explicitly about patience and "not accepting good enough" — pure Burkean modeling | all (ethos) |

## 2. Cooking / Food Science

**Best for technique + the WHY: Good Eats (science) and Jacques Pépin (technique).** ATK is the systematic-testing middle; Julia Child the heritage/joy pick.

| # | Title | Run / Count | Why | Projects |
|---|-------|-------------|-----|----------|
| 8 | **Good Eats** (Alton Brown) | 1999–2012 (+Reloaded/Returns 2019–21), 16 seasons, 256 eps | Every recipe is a science lesson — Maillard, protein denaturing, ratios; tutor hangs chemistry and fraction lessons directly on episodes. Top pick for cookbook + STEM crossover | cookbook, weather (states of matter) |
| 9 | **America's Test Kitchen** (PBS) | 2001–, 26 seasons | "Why this recipe works": controlled-variable testing, equipment science — scientific method applied to cooking; the recipe-testing workflow for the family cookbook | cookbook |
| 10 | **The French Chef** (Julia Child) | 1963–73, ~206 eps | Foundational technique, unflappable humor; models recovering from mistakes gracefully ("never apologize") | cookbook |
| 11 | **Jacques Pépin: Fast Food My Way / Essential Pépin** | 2004/2008/2011, ~26 eps each | Finest knife-skills and technique instruction ever televised; narrates the WHY of every motion; recipes a 12-year-old can replicate | cookbook |
| 12 | **Townsends** (YouTube) | 2009–, hundreds | 18th-century open-hearth cooking + log-cabin building; history-meets-food, family-run, gentle | cookbook, garden (heirloom crops) |
| 13 | **Baking with Julia** (PBS) | 1996–98, ~39 eps | Master bakers on bread/pastry fundamentals — baker's percentages = ratio math | cookbook |

## 3. Farming / Homesteading / Gardening

**The BBC historic farm series are the crown jewels of the whole channel** — Goodman/Langlands/Ginn live a full year on a period farm with period tools. Building, husbandry, food preservation, weather-dependence, and history in exactly the right register.

| # | Title | Run / Count | Why | Projects |

## Scheduling notes
- **Anchor blocks**: BBC farm series (evening family hour), Woodwright's/New Yankee (afternoon shop block), Good Eats + ATK (pre-dinner cooking block).
- **Filler (10–25m)**: Ask This Old House segments, How It's Made, Primitive Technology, Townsends, Paul Sellers shorts.
- **Tutor tie-ins**: Good Eats → fractions/chemistry; ATK → scientific method; New Yankee/Sellers → measurement & geometry; Mr. Wizard + MythBusters → weather instrumentation; Victorian Farm → history units.
- **yt-dlp archive targets**: Paul Sellers, Essential Craftsman (Spec House playlist), Justin Rhodes (chicken/coop playlists), Townsends, Primitive Technology, GBH Open Vault Crockett's Victory Garden, New Yankee Workshop official channel, Time Team official.
