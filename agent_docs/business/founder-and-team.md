# Why Aleks Clark?

## Founder-market fit

Aleks Clark has experienced the problem from three positions:

1. **As a homeschooled student:** he was homeschooled in grades K–7, 9, and 10 while his family lived in Peru with limited U.S.-accredited options. A typical day was about three hours, with flexibility to work ahead.
2. **As a homeschooling parent using the standard model:** he homeschooled two daughters for three years through grades 5–8 using Abeka across all subjects.
3. **As the parent-builder of Primer:** he has begun using command-teacher and PrimerTV with his sixth-grade son while the broader subject tutoring, project, and assessment system is built.

As a student, Aleks benefited from flexibility, self-direction, reading, and learning by doing. The transition back to school was academically easy and socially harder. As a parent, he found Abeka academically rigorous but inflexible: mastered work still had to be completed, assessment could be gamed, mastery was inferred mostly from grades, and some learning overemphasized recall. Grading, assessment supervision, record-keeping, and adapting projects required more than ten parent hours in a normal week.

The emotional burden matters too. The parent repeatedly becomes “the bad guy” enforcing routine work. Primer is intended to let the parent set direction, worldview, and guardrails while the system consistently enforces the daily standard. It also protects childhood time from low-quality, passive screen consumption by directing students toward reading, investigation, and making.

Primer is not an abstract attempt to apply AI to education. It is being built inside the workflow it is intended to improve, but current family use is early and must not be presented as outcome evidence.

## Technical fit

Aleks is a staff-level software engineer with experience in AI development infrastructure, distributed systems, cloud platforms, product engineering, reliability, and technical leadership.

Most directly, he spent five years at **eBackpack**, a learning management system focused on ease of use. His work included:

- operating a platform serving more than half a million students, approximately 60,000 requests per minute, and 99.9% uptime;
- building Go microservice infrastructure with 4–6 ms response times on critical endpoints;
- optimizing MongoDB and PostgreSQL workloads;
- building and operating petabyte-scale file storage;
- refactoring the frontend into TypeScript and Angular;
- improving product delivery speed and reducing unnecessary API traffic.

At eBackpack, Aleks directly built SIS integrations, OneDrive for Business integration, and assessment-platform capabilities. He integrated Skyward, PowerSchool, and Google Classroom for rosters and grades; implemented OAuth 1 and 2 with Google and Microsoft; tagged assessments to Common Core standards; and supported Common Cartridge content. His broader integration and platform experience includes:

- low-latency, failure-tolerant third-party API integrations;
- OpenAPI-documented REST platforms used by multiple product teams;
- identity-adjacent and secure-data systems;
- React, TypeScript, Go, Ruby on Rails, Kotlin, Python, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, AWS, and GCP;
- production systems serving demanding commercial and government customers;
- multi-agent AI development infrastructure integrated across Slack, GitHub, CI/CD, and production.

This makes SIS, SSO, LMS, roster, gradebook, and content-system integration familiar product and engineering work. It does not eliminate the need to implement and certify each school connector, but it substantially reduces founder execution risk around institutional interoperability.

## Why this combination matters

Education founders often have either classroom insight without the ability to build a complex platform, or software experience without sustained proximity to the learning problem. Aleks combines:

- lived experience as a homeschool student;
- repeated experience as a homeschooling parent;
- active use of Primer with his own child;
- five years building an LMS;
- staff-level platform and integration engineering;
- the ability to operate a complex product across AI, application, data, and infrastructure layers.

The resulting founder thesis is:

> The scarce resource is not content. It is sustained, adult-directed attention that diagnoses the student, holds a consistent standard across subjects, and leaves an auditable record of what was learned. Aleks has lived the gap, used the standard alternatives, built LMS infrastructure, and is now building and using the system he wanted as both student and parent.

## Why Primer can enter schools

The school expansion is not a founder pivot into an unfamiliar market. Aleks has already built LMS infrastructure and integrations. Primer can begin as:

- a family-owned supplementary LMS used alongside public or private school;
- a bounded school tutoring, intervention, enrichment, or project programme;
- an LTI-launched supplementary LMS, beginning with Clever SSO and expanding to roster and grade synchronization;
- eventually, a primary LMS where Primer has earned the right to replace more conventional workflows.

The founder's technical familiarity makes earlier supplementary pilots realistic. Privacy, child safety, accessibility, procurement, implementation support, and efficacy remain distinct company capabilities that must be built deliberately.

## What the founding team still needs

Founder fit does not substitute for a multidisciplinary team. Primer needs:

- a grades 5–8 curriculum and assessment leader;
- learning-science and research-design expertise;
- child-safety and student-privacy ownership;
- subject-matter reviewers for each launch domain;
- school implementation and education go-to-market expertise;
- accessibility and institutional security support.

The strongest team story is not that Aleks can do every job. It is that he brings unusual problem proximity and the technical range to build the platform, while adding the educational, research, safety, and commercial disciplines required to prove and scale it.
