# Section content specification

## Status language

Use only these product-state labels:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `LIVE` | Demonstrable now in a real environment |
| `IN DEVELOPMENT` | Active implementation with a defined owner and near-term milestone |
| `PLANNED` | Designed or intended, but not demonstrable |
| `HYPOTHESIS` | Business, pricing, learning, or adoption assumption awaiting data |
| `EVIDENCE NEEDED` | Claim cannot be made externally yet |

Do not use `beta`, `proven`, `validated`, or `traction` unless defined with a metric and date.

## Section matrix

| Section | Core claim | Required artifact | Required data | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hero | Mastery should determine progression | Product mark and concise system line | None | No outcome claim |
| Problem | Individual attention does not scale | Family/classroom comparison | Parent-time example; tutoring research link | Founder experience is illustrative |
| Product | Primer is an LMS that teaches | System diagram | Launch responsibilities | Most loop elements planned |
| Demo | Primer diagnoses, corrects, records, and revisits | Live capture or labeled target prototype | Standard IDs and evidence event | Never imply prototype is live |
| Teaching | Habits and transfer distinguish the method | Four concrete examples | Linked project/guard definitions | Mechanism, not efficacy |
| Evidence | Prior research supports the hypothesis | Three-tier evidence visual | 0.288 SD meta-analysis; pilot plan | No Primer outcome yet |
| Market | Family-first can support a large company | Interactive bottom-up model | Source-year populations and price scenarios | TAM is not SAM/forecast |
| GTM | Consumer use opens institutional adoption | Adoption ladder | Buyer/proof/integration per rung | Timelines unvalidated |
| Competition | Primer integrates tutoring, LMS, projects, and evidence | Category matrix | Official-product sources | No unverifiable checkmarks |
| Founder | Aleks has lived and built the category | Timeline and integration proof | Resume-backed scale/integrations | Current team is one person |
| State | Real foundation, very early use | Release board/screens | Hours, spend, usage start | No traction language |
| Model | Subscription plus usage can align price and inference | Pricing hypothesis card | Usage-cost model later | No margin claim yet |
| Ask | Capital builds product and proof | 18-month milestone map | Bottom-up hiring, compute, research, compliance, and contingency model | Tie each role and cost line to a milestone |

## Copy blocks

### Hero

**Eyebrow**

`AN INSTRUCTIONAL LMS FOR MASTERY`

**Headline**

`A student moves on when they can explain and use the skill.`

**Subhead**

`Primer gives each learner persistent, standards-aligned tutoring and gives the adult the evidence behind every mastery claim.`

**Proof line**

`Family-first · Supplementary school entry · Grades 5–8`

### Problem

**Headline**

`The curriculum can scale. Attention cannot.`

**Body**

`A package can assign the next page, and an LMS can collect it. Neither notices the first wrong step, insists on a complete correction, changes tomorrow's plan, and checks the skill again in a new context.`

**Founder annotation**

`Using a rigorous all-subject homeschool package still required more than ten parent hours each week and made mastery difficult to distinguish from completion.`

### Product

**Headline**

`An LMS whose primary job is teaching.`

**Body**

`Primer owns curriculum planning, assessment evaluation, and the longitudinal standards record. Specialist tutors and cross-subject guards use that memory to decide what the learner should do next.`

### Beachhead

**Headline**

`One learning system, two immediate family jobs.`

**Homeschool card**

`Preserve parent authority while reducing routine planning, enforcement, grading, and record-keeping.`

**Remediation card**

`Ingest the school's syllabus, identify the missing prerequisites, and focus instruction until progress appears on independent assessments and school work.`

### Evidence

**Headline**

`The mechanism is researched. The product still has to prove itself.`

**Research metric**

`+0.288 SD`

**Metric label**

`Average effect across 89 randomized preK–12 tutoring trials, not a Primer outcome.`

**Proof-plan statement**

`Primer's first failure test is simple: if learners do not progress on independently scored, state-aligned standards, the system is not working.`

### Market

**Headline**

`Start with a tutor. Expand into the education stack.`

**Expansion scenarios**

- `BASE · 3.4M modeled homeschool learners × $600 = $2.04B`
- `CORE · 14.61M public grades 5–8 learners × $1,200 = $17.53B`
- `PREMIER · 631,672 NAIS learners × $3,600 = $2.27B`
- `PREMIER PRIVATE · 1.16M learners in $15K+ tuition schools × $3,600 = $4.17B`

**Inline caveat**

`Overlapping expansion layers. Do not sum. Scenarios are not SAM, forecasts, or current revenue.`

### Founder

**Headline**

`Built by a homeschool student, homeschool parent, and LMS engineer.`

**Body**

`Aleks Clark was homeschooled in grades K–7, 9, and 10, later used Abeka to homeschool two daughters for three years, and has begun using Primer with his sixth-grade son. He spent five years building eBackpack for more than half a million students.`

**Technical strip**

`Skyward · PowerSchool · Google Classroom · OneDrive · OAuth · Common Core · Common Cartridge`

### Current state

**Headline**

`A real foundation. An unproven company.`

**Body**

`Command-teacher, PrimerTV, LMS core infrastructure, and the Ultralogical agent platform are demonstrable. Subject tutoring, projects, standards assessment, pricing, retention, and learning outcomes remain to be built or measured.`

### Ask

**Headline**

`Working target: a $3.5 million pre-seed for 18 months.`

**Subhead**

`A three-person senior team, paid $300,000 cash salary each with strong benefits, plus the inference, research, production, compliance, and pilot work required to reach seed readiness: 1,500 paying learners, $900K ARR run rate, 85% term retention, independently measured learning, and 70% post-credit gross margin.`

**Compensation annotation**

`Cash-forward compensation recruits staff-level owners for product, education, and platform outcomes. The financing model shows salary, benefits, equity, and capitalization as one plan.`

**Publication block**

`EVIDENCE NEEDED: quantified customers, revenue, retention, learning outcomes, option grants, total dilution, and next-round milestones.`

### Business model ladder

**Headline**

`One learner record. More educational responsibility.`

**Base**

`$50/month · Two or three subjects · Tutoring, assessment, mastery evidence`

**Core**

`$100/month · Full core academics · Planning, cross-subject reinforcement, projects, portfolio`

**Premier**

`$300/month · Customized full-spectrum education · Managed content, advanced support, and higher-touch reporting`

**Institutional annotation**

`Public schools buy targeted intervention at approximately $1,200 per supported learner annually. Elite schools buy parent access, learning support, or tuition-embedded academic capacity, not commodity software.`

## Visual requirements

### Product loop

Must show:

- adult authority entering the system;
- standards/evidence memory persisting across sessions;
- student correction loop;
- transfer into another context;
- adult review for exceptions.

Do not render the LLM as the center of the system.

### Market calculator

Every displayed value includes:

- source organization;
- source year;
- observed versus modeled tag;
- arithmetic formula;
- overlap warning.

### Founder timeline

Use no childhood or family photographs without explicit consent. A geographic marker for Peru and product/integration artifacts can carry the story without exposing children.

### Product screenshots

Redact all student information. Use synthetic data unless explicit consent and a documented reason support real artifacts.

## Tone

- Direct, precise, and candid.
- Avoid “revolutionize,” “disrupt,” “personalized at scale,” and “AI-powered” as unsupported category language.
- Prefer “designed to” over “does” for planned mechanisms.
- Prefer “working hypothesis” over “business model” where no transaction has occurred.
- State failure conditions and risks alongside upside.

## Claims requiring footnotes

- homeschool population and growth;
- private/public enrollment;
- parent spending;
- tutoring effect sizes;
- mastery/adaptive evidence;
- competitor features and prices;
- eBackpack scale and uptime;
- any school integration standard or compliance deadline;
- all TAM calculations.
