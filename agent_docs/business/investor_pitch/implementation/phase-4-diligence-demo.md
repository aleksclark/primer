# Phase 4 — Diligence routes and product demo

## Goal

Add the depth investors expect after the primary narrative earns interest: market assumptions, evidence, school readiness, founder proof, product state, and a truthful demonstration.

## Routes

### `/demo`

Show two explicitly separated areas.

**Live product surfaces**

- command-teacher capture;
- PrimerTV capture;
- LMS core/admin capture;
- Ultralogical platform diagram.

**Target instructional experience**

- syllabus ingestion;
- prerequisite diagnosis;
- student attempt;
- reasoning prompt;
- habit correction;
- revision;
- mastery evidence;
- scheduled transfer check.

Label the second flow `TARGET EXPERIENCE` until it is demonstrable. Use synthetic learner data.

### `/market`

Include:

- source-year market tables;
- Base/Core/Premier expansion layers;
- family versus institutional contracting models;
- premium private-school cohorts;
- tutoring price comparisons;
- public intervention pricing rationale;
- international/microschool/special-support opportunity map;
- overlap/deduplication methodology;
- downloadable or printable assumption summary.

### `/evidence`

Include:

- tutoring meta-analysis;
- mastery-learning evidence;
- adaptive-learning evidence;
- Bloom caveat;
- Primer measurement-validity plan;
- feasibility/comparative study stages;
- seed learning thresholds;
- null/adverse result policy;
- full source register.

### `/schools`

Include:

- family-to-school supplementary motion;
- targeted public intervention;
- elite independent and boarding learning support;
- LTI/Clever/OneRoster roadmap;
- Blackbaud/Veracross integration strategy;
- privacy, COPPA, FERPA, CIPA support, accessibility, DPA, SOC 2 roadmap;
- public versus private buying motion;
- primary-LMS path after de facto adoption.

### `/company`

Include:

- founder timeline;
- eBackpack and integration execution details;
- current team;
- 18-month role ownership;
- hiring principles;
- founder-risk mitigation through educator ownership and product evidence;
- contact and references process.

### `/diligence`

Public index for:

- product state;
- market model;
- funding plan;
- seed-readiness economics;
- material risks;
- compliance roadmap;
- source register;
- optional gated data-room link.

Do not expose private contracts, family data, detailed security architecture, or cap-table documents publicly.

## Product demo implementation

Prefer a deterministic scripted demonstration over a live model call for the investor site:

- repeatable;
- safe;
- fast;
- no inference cost or provider outage;
- exact educational behavior reviewed;
- accessible transcript available;
- each step linked to the mechanism it proves.

A separate private live demo can be used in meetings when stable.

## Investor familiarity

Deep routes should resemble a well-organized data-room index:

- clear document title;
- updated date;
- concise executive summary;
- ruled section navigation;
- source and download controls;
- no marketing animation;
- printable layouts.

System C makes diligence feel like a structured record, which is directly aligned with the design system.

## Tests

- route loading and direct-link refresh;
- no private/gated asset included in public build output;
- demo transcript and visual steps remain synchronized;
- all screenshots contain synthetic/redacted data;
- print styles for market/evidence/diligence;
- source links checked;
- route-level metadata/Open Graph cards;
- canonical URLs and sitemap;
- noindex support for staging/gated environments.

## Exit criteria

- an interested investor can answer core diligence questions without a founder walkthrough;
- product state and target experience cannot be confused;
- market layers and package tiers are reproducible;
- school expansion requirements are concrete;
- public and private diligence boundaries are documented and enforced.
