## Founder story

1. What years and grades were you homeschooled, and what did a normal week look like?
   - Answer: k-7, 9, 10. In a normal week I'd have ~3 hours of homeschooling per day, depending on if I got stuck on something or not. Often I would work ahead so I could have days off

2. Why did your family choose homeschooling?
   - Answer: necessity - we lived in Peru so limited US-accredited options

3. What worked particularly well for you as a homeschooled student?
   - Answer: flexibility and self-directed learning

4. What did homeschooling fail to provide or make unnecessarily difficult?
   - Answer: encountering wrong answers in the answer key was frustrating because it meant having to do an additional exercise each time I got an exercise "wrong"

5. What was the transition into school after 10th grade like academically and socially?
   - Answer: academically it was a piece of cake, socially it was rougher

6. Which parts of your own education most directly shaped Primer's design?
   - Answer: I read a lot, and learned on my own a lot, by doing. I want students to be able to do that.

## Homeschooling your children

    - Answer: being "the bad guy" with your kids is a drag, I'd rather be an enabler and the system be the bad guy. Additionally, time is very easy to waste in childhood, especially being glued to a device consuming low-quality, short content that doesn't actually teach you anything or help you learn

## Product conviction

31. What is the single problem Primer must solve better than every alternative?
    - Answer: individualized learning and habit-forming, with evidence of mastery, with very low educator burden

32. Why is an LMS the right product category for Primer rather than only an AI tutor?
    - Answer: Education is a long-run endeavor, we need to know where we've been to know where we're going. Additionally, overly-narrow instruction is wasteful when we can teach cross-cutting skills/standards in a single activity/session

33. Which LMS responsibilities must Primer own at launch?
    - Answer: curriculum planning, assessment evaluation, standards/mastery records

34. Which LMS responsibilities can wait?
    - Answer: external SIS/SSO login

35. What should remain under adult judgment even if the AI can automate it?
    - Answer: content guardrails that are strictly enforced, balance of learning via instruction vs project vs investigation, overarching worldview that informs curriculum planning

36. What should Primer never optimize for?
    - Answer: pointless learning

37. What is the strongest reason a family would stop using Primer?
    - Answer: preference for traditional homeschooling/public schools, fear of student autonomy

38. What belief about education would you retain even if it reduced the addressable market?
    - Answer: that the *way* students are taught is as much a lesson as is the content itself

## Evidence and safety

79. What independent assessment can measure the first product's learning effect?
    - Answer: state standards testing (TCAP, etc.) - potentially import old test content and do micro-assessments

80. What outcome would convince you Primer is not working?
    - Answer: lack of progression in tested standards

81. How will mastery decisions be checked against human reviewers?
    - Answer: minimally - the point is to build a system that can autonomously evaluate mastery. Spot checking potentially used to identify novel cheating patterns, heuristics to call out abnormal jumps in mastery

82. Which decisions always require adult approval?
    - Answer: abandoning an avenue of mastery or a project

83. What student information will never be used for model training?
    - Answer: no student information will be used for model training by default. In the future we may consider a discounted "beta program" that uses anonymized data to improve the system

84. What is the default retention and deletion policy for family accounts?
    - Answer: raw session data retained for 1 month for all accounts. For inactive/deleted accounts, media will be deleted after 3 months, LMS data retained forever. deletion requests will be immediately honored, retaining only accounting/billing data (this will include usage statistics that drive billing)

85. What child-safety events should trigger an alert or stop the session?
    - Answer: going off-topic - basically acting as a whitelist, we evaluate a given session against the topic that is supposed to be under instruction, and halt/alert if there is deviation. Also time limits, sessions should not be overly long.
