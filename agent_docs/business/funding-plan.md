# Pre-seed funding and operating plan

## Target

Primer is targeting an **18-month pre-seed round** supporting:

- founder Aleks Clark at **$300,000 annual cash salary**;
- one or two additional high-leverage employees at approximately **$300,000 annual cash salary each**;
- strong health and other benefits;
- cash-forward compensation paired with a focused employee option pool;
- product development, model inference, media, production infrastructure, research, compliance, and customer discovery.

This compensation strategy prioritizes experienced people who can independently own broad functions. Salary, benefits, and equity are designed as one coherent package that supports focused execution with low coordination overhead.

## Important dilution distinction

Cash-forward compensation supports a focused **option pool** while the financing plan funds the full employment package. The company will model financing dilution and option-pool dilution together so investors can evaluate the complete capitalization plan.

The relevant comparison is:

`total dilution = financing dilution + option-pool dilution + future financing dilution`

The operating thesis is that a compact senior team reaches product, learning, and revenue milestones with fewer handoffs and less management overhead.

## Compensation model

Use a 30% fully-loaded employment factor for planning. This covers employer payroll taxes, strong benefits, payroll/PEO costs, and ordinary employee overhead. Equipment and unusual recruiting costs remain separate.

| Team | 18-month cash salaries | 30% employment load | Equipment/setup | 18-month people cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Founder + 1 employee | $900,000 | $270,000 | $6,000 | **$1,176,000** |
| Founder + 2 employees | $1,350,000 | $405,000 | $9,000 | **$1,764,000** |

Each role is budgeted at $300,000 annual cash salary plus strong benefits. This creates a consistent compensation framework for recruiting staff-level talent capable of owning major company outcomes.

## Recommended fundraising target

Use **$3.5 million for 18 months** as the working pre-seed target if Primer intends to hire two additional people early. The amount follows the bottom-up operating plan: a compact senior team, separately modeled product and infrastructure costs, and explicit month-18 seed-readiness milestones.

Use **$2.25 million** if the second employee is contingent on product validation or hired later. Use up to **$4.25 million** only when the model, research, compliance, and production plans justify the full operating envelope.

The $3.5 million pre-seed is tied to a concrete milestone plan rather than a generic runway target. The target economics are defined in [seed-readiness-economics.md](seed-readiness-economics.md).

## Team design

The first two additions must eliminate whole categories of founder risk, not merely add capacity.

### Role 1: educator/product owner

Owns:

- launch curriculum and instructional requirements;
- assessment quality and mastery criteria;
- design-partner workflow;
- product prioritization from educator and parent evidence;
- external curriculum/research reviewers.

### Role 2: high-leverage builder

Likely an experienced product/platform engineer who can independently own one or more of:

- learner application and mobile experience;
- agent/tutoring runtime;
- inference instrumentation and cost controls;
- institutional integrations;
- production reliability, privacy, and security implementation.

Sales should remain founder-led until the company has retention and a repeatable customer profile. Specialist legal, research, accessibility, curriculum, and security work can be contracted before each becomes a full-time role.

## Equity strategy

The option pool should provide meaningful long-term alignment while reflecting the cash-forward compensation package and the limited 18-month hiring plan.

The plan should specify:

- expected grant range by role;
- four-year vesting with a one-year cliff;
- total option pool required for the 18-month hiring plan;
- whether refresh grants are expected;
- the trade between salary and equity offered to each candidate;
- investor dilution under the proposed instrument and cap.

For modeling, test a **5–8% near-term option pool** rather than assuming the 10–15% often used for larger early hiring plans. Final pool size should follow actual hiring commitments and financing negotiation.

## Milestones purchased by the round

The round should buy evidence, not merely runway.

### Product

- paid remediation workflow from syllabus ingestion through independent reassessment;
- curriculum planning, assessment evaluation, and standards/mastery records;
- production tutor and learner experience;
- instrumented inference cost per learner;
- reliable family administration and data controls;
- selected mobile experience;
- LTI and Clever SSO pilot capability after family proof.

### Customer

- a defined launch segment and repeatable acquisition channel;
- paid family pilots;
- demonstrated value within one or two sessions;
- 4-, 8-, and 12-week retention cohorts;
- at least one bounded supplementary school pilot if family evidence supports it.

### Learning

- validated mastery-scoring agreement with independent reviewers;
- measurable progression on an external state-aligned assessment;
- documented dosage and implementation fidelity;
- evidence of parent time saved;
- explicit null/failure criteria.

### Economics

- 1,000 paying learners as the minimum floor and 1,500 as the target;
- $600K–$900K ARR run rate at the working $50 monthly price;
- 85%+ target 12-week paid retention;
- observed model and infrastructure COGS by usage percentile;
- 70%+ target post-credit gross margin, with 60% as the minimum floor;
- ≤$175 target blended CAC and ≤5-month gross-profit payback;
- a viable included-usage policy at the working $50 monthly price;
- measured support burden and onboarding cost;
- independently measured standards progression and mastery-scoring reliability.

## Pitch framing

Use:

> Primer is raising a $3.5 million pre-seed for 18 months. The round funds a three-person, senior team with cash-forward compensation and a small option footprint, plus the model, research, production, and compliance work required to reach 1,500 paying learners, a $900,000 ARR run rate, 85% term retention, independently measured standards progression, and 70% post-credit gross margin.

Do not use:

> We doubled payroll to estimate development costs.

Investors should see the actual cost drivers, each role's ownership mandate, and the evidence the round will produce.
