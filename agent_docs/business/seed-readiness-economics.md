# Seed-readiness economics

## Purpose

The current round is a **$3.5 million pre-seed for 18 months**. The economics below define what Primer should demonstrate by month 18 to justify a subsequent seed round.

The objective is not profitability. It is to prove that Primer can acquire and retain paying families, improve independently measured learning, and deliver each learner at a credible gross margin before scaling the team and entering schools more broadly.

## Seed thesis at month 18

Primer should be able to say:

> Families pay approximately $50 per learner per month, learners remain active across an academic term, independently measured standards mastery improves, and the service delivers at least 70% gross margin with a credible path above 75%. We have enough repeatable acquisition and cohort evidence to invest seed capital in distribution, mobile, curriculum breadth, and school integrations.

## Recommended target case

| Metric | Month-18 target | Minimum credible floor | Why it matters |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Paying learners | **1,500** | 1,000 | Enough cohort depth to test retention, usage, support, and learning across varied families |
| MRR at $50 | **$75,000** | $50,000 | Demonstrates meaningful paid demand |
| ARR run rate | **$900,000** | $600,000 | Exceeds the current $300K–$500K generic seed expectation while acknowledging edtech's proof burden |
| 12-week paid retention | **≥85%** | ≥75% | Shows value across an academic term rather than novelty |
| Monthly logo churn after onboarding | **≤4%** | ≤6% | Supports a viable subscription, with seasonality reported separately |
| Gross margin, post-credit | **≥70%** | ≥60% | Demonstrates that inference-heavy tutoring can become an attractive software business |
| COGS per learner/month | **≤$15** | ≤$20 | At $50 price, corresponds to 70% and 60% gross margins |
| Inference COGS | **≤$8/learner/month median** | ≤$12 | Leaves room for cloud, media, storage, support, and payment costs |
| 95th-percentile COGS | **≤$25/learner/month** | ≤$35 with overage/cap | Prevents power users from silently destroying margin |
| Blended CAC | **≤$175** | ≤$250 | Supports sub-six-month gross-profit payback at the target margin |
| CAC payback | **≤5 months** | ≤8 months | Preserves capital efficiency in a consumer channel |
| Gross-profit LTV:CAC | **≥3×** | ≥2× with improving trend | Shows acquisition can scale beyond founder referrals |
| Core onboarding labor | **≤30 minutes/account** | ≤60 minutes | Validates the low-educator-burden and scalable-support thesis |
| Ongoing support | **≤20 minutes/learner/month** | ≤40 minutes | Prevents the product from becoming disguised human tutoring |
| Activation | **≥70%** complete first value loop in 48 hours | ≥50% | Confirms one-to-two-session time-to-value |
| Learning outcome | **≥0.20 SD** or pre-specified equivalent | statistically credible positive progression | Establishes pedagogical market fit on an independent measure |
| Mastery scoring | **≥90% agreement** with blind expert review | ≥85% | Supports autonomous assessment without unacceptable false mastery |
| School proof | **1–3 paid bounded pilots** | one credible design partner | Shows a path beyond direct family revenue without distracting from it |

## Revenue scenarios at month 18

Seed readiness should be measured on the Base package, not dependent on unproven upsell. Assume $50 monthly revenue per learner:

| Case | Paying learners | MRR | ARR run rate | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Failure/insufficient proof | 250 | $12,500 | $150,000 | Useful product learning, but difficult institutional seed case |
| Early signal | 500 | $25,000 | $300,000 | Generic seed revenue floor, but requires exceptional retention and learning proof |
| Minimum credible seed | 1,000 | $50,000 | $600,000 | Supports a seed process if cohorts, outcomes, and margins are strong |
| **Target** | **1,500** | **$75,000** | **$900,000** | Stronger proof that the family wedge can support a venture business |
| Stretch | 2,500 | $125,000 | $1,500,000 | Excellent signal if not purchased with unsustainable CAC or support |

Revenue must be recurring paid usage. Free pilots, annual contract value not yet collected, grants, and forward-looking school contracts should be reported separately. Core ($100) and Premier ($300) may be tested during the pre-seed, but the target case does not require them to make the Base economics work.

## Gross-margin model

At the working $50 monthly price:

| Gross margin | Maximum total COGS per learner/month | Gross profit per learner/month |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 50% | $25.00 | $25.00 |
| 60% | $20.00 | $30.00 |
| 65% | $17.50 | $32.50 |
| **70%** | **$15.00** | **$35.00** |
| 75% | $12.50 | $37.50 |

### COGS definition

Include:

- model inference and evaluation calls;
- learner-attributable cloud compute;
- storage, content delivery, media processing, and streaming;
- third-party APIs used to deliver the service;
- payment processing;
- variable customer support and onboarding labor;
- any human curriculum/tutoring labor required per learner.

Do not hide inference, support, or production operations in R&D. Show margins both before and after cloud/model credits, with **post-credit/list-rate economics** as the investor benchmark.

### Target $15 monthly COGS envelope

| Cost | Target per learner/month |
|---|---:|
| Inference and model evaluation | $8.00 |
| Cloud, storage, media, observability | $2.50 |
| Payment processing | $1.75 |
| Variable support/onboarding | $2.00 |
| Other learner-variable services | $0.75 |
| **Total** | **$15.00** |

The model must also report median, 75th, 90th, and 95th-percentile usage. An average can conceal a small cohort of unprofitable users.

## Retention model

The 12-week target is a direct milestone because Primer should become valuable across a school term. A 12-week retention rate does not by itself establish annual retention.

If churn were constant, 85% retention every 12 weeks implies approximately:

- 94.7% monthly retention;
- 5.3% monthly churn;
- 52.2% annual retention.

That is adequate evidence for seed, but not a mature consumer subscription. The stronger target is **90% 12-week retention**, equivalent under a constant-churn simplification to about 3.45% monthly churn and 65.6% annual retention.

Report retention by:

- homeschool versus supplementary remediation;
- monthly versus annual plan;
- grade and subject;
- acquisition channel;
- activation status;
- academic calendar versus true cancellation;
- paid cohort, excluding free pilots.

## CAC, payback, and LTV

At $50 monthly revenue and 70% gross margin, monthly gross profit is $35.

### CAC payback

| CAC | Gross-profit payback |
|---:|---:|
| $100 | 2.9 months |
| **$175** | **5.0 months** |
| $250 | 7.1 months |
| $350 | 10.0 months |

The target is blended CAC at or below $175 and paid CAC at or below $250. Founder referrals and organic word of mouth should be reported separately from repeatable paid or partner channels.

### Simplified gross-profit LTV

Using `monthly gross profit ÷ monthly churn`:

| Monthly churn | Simplified gross-profit LTV | Maximum CAC for 3× LTV:CAC |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 3% | $1,167 | $389 |
| 4% | $875 | $292 |
| 5% | $700 | $233 |
| 7% | $500 | $167 |

This formula is only a directional seed metric. It assumes constant churn and ignores discounting, seasonality, expansion, pauses, and changing gross margin. Investors should receive observed cohort curves, not only a derived ratio.

## Acquisition pace

If paid launch begins in month 7, reaching 1,500 active paying learners by month 18 requires approximately **125 net active learner additions per month** across the final twelve months. Reaching the 1,000-learner floor requires approximately **84 net additions per month**.

Because churn compounds, gross monthly additions must exceed these figures. The operating model should show:

- new paid learners;
- reactivations;
- cancellations;
- pauses for academic-calendar reasons;
- net active learners;
- channel-specific CAC;
- conversion from qualified lead to paid activation.

A credible month-18 target curve should accelerate rather than assume a linear launch from month one.

## Learning economics

The seed case needs both commercial and pedagogical unit economics.

### Cost per successful learner

Track:

`acquisition cost + onboarding cost + 12-week COGS + allocated assessment cost`

At target economics:

- CAC: $175;
- onboarding/support included in monthly COGS;
- 12-week COGS: approximately $45;
- external assessment allocation: target ≤$30;
- total cost to acquire and serve through 12 weeks: approximately **$250**.

Three months of revenue produce $150, so cash payback does not occur within the first term at $175 CAC. At $35 monthly gross profit, payback occurs around month five. This is acceptable if 12-week retention predicts continued paid use.

### Outcome target

Use an independently scored, state-aligned assessment with a pre-registered primary outcome. A **0.20 SD target** is large by modern education-RCT benchmarks and should be treated as a stretch target, not assumed. The minimum credible result is a statistically and educationally meaningful positive effect or progression against a valid comparison, with dosage and attrition disclosed.

Also report:

- percentage of learners advancing at least one pre-specified mastery level;
- false-mastery rate;
- time and cost per mastered standard;
- learning gain per active hour;
- parent time saved;
- subgroup results only when sample sizes support them.

## Capital efficiency

The pre-seed round is $3.5 million. If Primer reaches $900,000 ARR run rate by month 18, the ratio of pre-seed capital raised to ending ARR is **3.9×**. At the $600,000 floor it is **5.8×**. These are not standard burn multiples because the company begins near zero revenue and the round funds product creation and efficacy research.

For the final six months before seed fundraising, track a conventional net burn multiple:

`net cash burn ÷ net new ARR`

Target:

- **<3×** by the final quarter;
- clear month-over-month improvement;
- a model showing how seed capital moves it below 2× as revenue scales.

A three-person team at the planned compensation level has high fixed burn. Seed investors must see that the team created disproportionate product, learning, and revenue evidence, not merely that runway was consumed.

## What would justify the seed round

Primer is seed-ready when all four statements are true:

1. **Demand:** at least 1,000, preferably 1,500, paying learners produce $600K–$900K ARR run rate.
2. **Retention:** paid cohorts persist across a 12-week term, with 75% as the floor and 85%+ as the target.
3. **Learning:** independent evidence shows meaningful standards progression and Primer's mastery decisions agree with expert reviewers.
4. **Economics:** post-credit gross margin is at least 60%, trending toward 70%+, with controlled power-user costs and sub-eight-month CAC payback.

A shortfall in one area can be offset only by exceptional proof elsewhere. Examples:

- 500 learners may be fundable with 90%+ term retention, strong independent effects, low CAC, and clear acceleration;
- 2,500 learners with poor retention or no learning evidence is not a strong education seed case;
- strong learning evidence with 40% gross margin requires a quantified path to pricing or inference improvement;
- school LOIs without family retention and product usage are not equivalent to traction.

## Expansion evidence for seed

In addition to Base economics, demonstrate that ARPU can expand without eroding retention or margins:

- at least 10% of eligible active families accept a Core $100/month offer in a controlled test;
- at least 20 paid families or one elite-school design partner validate Premier's $300/month value proposition;
- document which added capabilities, usage, support, and COGS distinguish each tier;
- measure expansion MRR separately from new-logo MRR;
- do not blend institutional annual contract value into consumer ARR.

These are validation targets, not required scale milestones. They show the seed investor that the $50 beachhead can expand toward full-spectrum education and premium private-school economics.

## Expected seed ask

If these targets are achieved, a subsequent seed round in the current market would likely be framed around **$4–6 million for 24 months**, funding:

- curriculum and subject expansion;
- mobile and learner experience;
- repeatable family distribution;
- institutional implementation and integrations;
- independent efficacy research;
- security, accessibility, and compliance maturity.

The exact seed size should follow the milestones and observed burn, not be predetermined now.
