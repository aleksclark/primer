# Primer logo generation prompt

Use the following as the first image-generation prompt. Generate broad directions before asking for polished mockups.

```text
Create a professional vector logo exploration for “Primer,” an AI-powered homeschool tutoring system for grades 6–8. Primer helps parents direct mastery-based learning while specialist AI tutors provide patient, rigorous instruction. The brand should feel like a precise instrument for serious learning: scholarly, disciplined, calm, humane, quietly optimistic, and technically capable.

Explore the tension between classical learning and modern tools. Develop a distinctive abstract symbol that can suggest foundation, illumination, ordered progress, dialogue, or a “primer” as both a foundational text and a catalyst. Favor intelligent geometric construction and meaningful negative space. Do not use literal open books, graduation caps, apples, owls, brains, sparkles, chat bubbles, robots, shields, crests, or the letter P placed plainly inside a circle.

Create 6 clearly different concept directions on one clean presentation sheet. For each direction show:
1. symbol alone,
2. horizontal symbol plus the exact word “Primer,”
3. one-color black version,
4. reversed white version on near-black charcoal #0B0F14,
5. a 24 px small-size test.

Use a restrained visual language with moderate geometry and excellent optical balance. Pair the mark with a highly legible, contemporary humanist or neo-grotesque wordmark. The identity must appeal to thoughtful parents and middle-school students without looking childish, corporate, futuristic, religious, elite-school heraldic, or like a generic SaaS company.

Use flat vector shapes only. No gradients, shadows, 3D effects, mockup photography, paper textures, slogans, extra words, or decorative background scenes. Start in monochrome so the idea carries the identity. Apply #5B8CFF only as a restrained accent after the monochrome concept works. Preserve generous clear space and show all concepts front-facing on a neutral grid.

Output style: senior identity designer’s vector concept board, crisp Bézier geometry, minimal, scalable, production-ready, white and charcoal background, 16:9 landscape, high resolution.
```

## Refinement prompt

After selecting one direction, attach it and use:

```text
Refine the attached Primer logo direction without changing its central idea. Correct optical alignment, Bézier curves, stroke weight, internal spacing, wordmark kerning, and symbol-to-wordmark proportion. Simplify any detail that fails below 24 px. Deliver a coherent family: horizontal lockup, stacked lockup, symbol, one-color version, reversed version, favicon, and app-icon-safe version. Show clear-space and minimum-size guidance. Use flat vector shapes only, no gradients, shadows, 3D, or mockups. Spell “Primer” exactly. Present on both #0B0F14 and #F7F8FA.
```

## Selection rubric

Score each concept from 1–5 on distinctiveness, small-size recognition, monochrome strength, parent credibility, student approachability, cross-platform flexibility, and ownership potential. Reject any concept that depends on explanation or resembles a common education-app icon.
